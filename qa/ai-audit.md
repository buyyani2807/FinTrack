# AI feature audit

## Verified on Buyyani's Enterprises (20 Oct)

Who owes me? returned the same ₹2,94,839.83 as the receivables tile and the four open bills. It did not invent a fifth customer.

## Not verified this pass

Daily business brief numbers, expense insights, credit score, bill-scan extraction, "describe a voucher in words", empty-company behaviour, a timeout, a rate limit, and whether a second company can appear in an answer. Company 1 is excluded from this audit.

## Product gaps, not runtime defects

No feature was found that files GST, posts a voucher from an AI answer without a human save, or answers from another company's books in the Buyyani check above. Those controls were not stress-tested.

## Accuracy rule

Any AI number that is not tied to a ledger, an open bill, or a cashbook row should be treated as unverified. The one comparison that was made matched the books.
